# Project instructions

## Purpose and scope

Help AI, RAG and agent developers preregister experiments and compare disclosed results with their original plans.

- Read README.md for the planned experience and verification boundaries.
- The product is exploratory. Its stack, architecture and MVP scope are undecided.
- Do not present planned functionality as implemented, deployed or validated by users.
- Keep payments, revenue splitting, bookings, token issuance, NFTs, betting and cryptocurrency prizes outside the product core.
- Distinguish blockchain commitments and validator checks from claims about external facts.
- Add development and verification commands only after a runnable scaffold exists and the commands can be checked.

## Project-specific boundary

Commitments do not prove actual execution, truthful results, fair scoring or registration of every experiment. Original files require separate storage.
