03
Verify a disclosure

        
Check a bundle directly, or check an on-chain announcement, its signer and the bundle. Bundles and files can be uploaded here or fetched from their own URLs; either way they are checked by hash.

        
This page reads raw transactions from Koios and verifies them itself: transaction hash, metadata hash and the registrant's signature, then a Mithril certificate for inclusion and block time. The first Mithril check in a browser takes about a minute; later checks reuse verified certificates.
